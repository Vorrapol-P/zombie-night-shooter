# Zombie Night — credits

Browser-based zombie-defense prototype. This deployment preserves the original game unchanged.

## Controls
- Mouse: aim; left click: shoot.
- R or right click: reload.
- A/D or arrow keys: move left/right behind the fence.
- Hold E: repair the fence section where you stand.
- P or Escape: pause/resume.
- Touch devices: tap to shoot; on-screen movement and repair controls; tap the ammo area to reload.

## Third-party components
- three.js r170, including GLTFLoader, SkeletonUtils and animation utilities — MIT; https://threejs.org/ ; served from jsDelivr.
- Animated Zombie by Quaternius — CC0; https://poly.pizza/m/jkrEvQZb8J (assets/zombie_a.glb).
- Zombie by Quaternius, Animated Enemies — CC0; https://poly.pizza/m/VlXjG0N8Eg (assets/zombie_b.glb).
- Background scene: creator-owned photo and derived scene layers.
- Weapon, effects and sounds: procedural.

No accounts, analytics, API keys or server-side services are required by this game. The best score is stored locally in the player's browser.
